 I am an **Assistant Professor** in Computer Science & Engineering at **Vasavi College of Engineering (Autonomous), Hyderabad**. I received my **Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering** from University College of Engineering, **Osmania University** (2024), where my dissertation, _"A Deep Learning-Based IDS Framework for Network Attack Detection and Classification"_, was supervised by **Prof. Ram Mohan Rao Kovvur**.

My research applies **deep learning to cybersecurity**: building intrusion detection systems that detect and classify network attacks, with a focus on DoS/DDoS attacks and IoT networks. My work has appeared in _Computers and Electrical Engineering_ (Elsevier, SCIE), the _Journal of Information & Knowledge Management_ (World Scientific), _Measurement: Sensors_ (Elsevier), and IEEE and Springer conference proceedings.

Qualified **GATE** in Computer Science & IT (2012, 2023) and Data Science & AI (2024).

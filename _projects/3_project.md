Denial-of-service (DoS) and distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks remain among the most disruptive network threats. This line of work studies **multi-class classification** of DoS and DDoS traffic with deep learning, and **feature selection** with the INBPOA optimisation algorithm to improve detection.

#### Related publications

- An Effective Deep Learning Based Multi-Class Classification of DoS and DDoS Attack Detection. _International Journal of Electrical and Computer Engineering Systems_, 14(4), 2023 (ESCI, Scopus). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.32985/ijeces.14.4.6)

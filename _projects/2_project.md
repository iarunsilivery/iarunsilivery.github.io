Internet of Things (IoT) networks connect many devices, which widens the attack surface. This work proposes a **hybrid intrusion detection** approach for IoT networks that extracts features along **two paths** before classification.

#### Related publications

- Dual-path Feature Extraction based Hybrid Intrusion Detection in IoT Networks. _Computers and Electrical Engineering_, Elsevier, Vol. 122, 2025 (SCIE, Scopus). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compeleceng.2024.109949)
- Intrusion detection models for IOT networks via deep learning approaches. _Measurement: Sensors_, Elsevier, 2023. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.measen.2022.100641)

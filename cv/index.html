Professional Summary

Assistant Professor at Vasavi College of Engineering (Autonomous), Hyderabad, with a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering from Osmania University. My research develops deep learning-based intrusion detection systems for network attack detection and classification, with a focus on DoS/DDoS attacks and IoT networks. I have published 6 journal articles and 4 conference papers, hold 3 published Indian patents and designs, and review for 9 international journals.
